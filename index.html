About Me

Empathy first.
Pixels second.

I'm a UI/UX and Product Designer based in Nigeria, passionate about solving real human problems through thoughtful, research-driven design. My work spans healthcare apps, health-tech dashboards, and agricultural e-commerce platforms.

With a background in English Education, I bring strong communication instincts to everything I design — making complex things simple, and simple things beautiful. I've trained with Google, HerTechTrail, and Tech4Dev, and I'm constantly growing.

I design for clarity, accessibility, and delight. I believe the best product experiences are invisible — they just feel right.
